SWK OS BRAND PACK

Master contains editable satin and flat SVGs with named crest, wordmark and
product-name groups. ForDark means the artwork is designed for dark surfaces.
ForLight is designed for white or pale surfaces. All logo and icon exports are
transparent and all lettering is outlined.

Logo includes stacked, horizontal and wordmark-only SVG and PNG versions. Icon contains
the standalone family crest. App_Icon contains branded dark and ivory tiles,
a 1024 px iOS asset catalog, and Android adaptive and legacy icon files.
Favicon contains SVG, PNG, multi-size ICO, and the branded touch icon.
The tiny browser favicon is shared across the SWK family.

The uploaded renders informed the crest, satin palette and layout. The crest
was redrawn as vector artwork and paired with the established SWK wordmark.
